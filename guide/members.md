> View and manage the members of your kennel. See profiles and switch members between active and inactive states.
{.is-info}

## Add a member

- **Required**: Short Name.
- **Status**: Active or Inactive.
- **Optional fields**: Full Name, Hash Name
- **Actions**: Save Member to create/update; Cancel to return to the list.
