## Runs
- **Filters**: Upcoming, Past, All.
- **Columns**: Run #, Date, Hare, Location, Status, Actions (edit/delete).
- **Quick actions**: Add Event, Schedule Run.
- **Status badges**: Scheduled, Published, or Hare & Location Needed to highlight gaps.
